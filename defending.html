Defending

Defending is an important part of soccer that involves preventing the opposing team from advancing and creating scoring opportunities. Good defenders use positioning, awareness, communication, and timing to protect their goal.

Defending Techniques

Defensive Stance at a 45-Degree Angle

Positioning your body at roughly a 45-degree angle helps you stay balanced while guiding an attacker away from dangerous areas. It also allows you to react more easily to changes in direction.

Jockeying and Not Diving Early

Jockeying involves staying in front of an attacker while maintaining a balanced position. Instead of rushing into a tackle, defenders remain patient and wait for a good opportunity to challenge.

Slide Tackle

A slide tackle is a defensive technique used to attempt to win the ball while sliding along the ground. It requires good timing and control to avoid committing a foul.

Forcing the Opponent onto Their Weak Foot

Defenders can position themselves to encourage an opponent to use their less comfortable foot. This can make it more difficult for the attacker to control, pass, or shoot the ball effectively.

Patience and Timing

Good defenders avoid rushing into challenges. By staying patient and observing the attacker, they can choose a better moment to attempt to win possession of the ball.

Goalside Defending

Goalside defending means staying between the attacker and your own goal. This makes it harder for the opposing player to get into a dangerous scoring position.
