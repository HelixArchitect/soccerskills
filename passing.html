Passing

Passing is one of the most important skills in soccer. It allows players to move the ball between teammates, maintain possession, create opportunities, and move around the opposing team's defense.

Passing Techniques

Lofted Long Ball

A lofted long ball is a pass that travels through the air over a longer distance. It can be used to reach teammates in open spaces or move the ball across the field.

Pinged Pass

A pinged pass is a firm, accurate pass that travels quickly over a longer distance. It is often used to switch play or reach a teammate in open space.

Chip Pass

A chip pass lifts the ball into the air to get it over an opponent. It can be useful when a defender is blocking a direct passing route.

No-Look Pass

A no-look pass is made while looking in a different direction from the intended target. It can disguise the pass and make it harder for opponents to anticipate where the ball is going.

One-Touch Passes

A one-touch pass is made immediately after receiving the ball, without taking an additional touch to control it. It helps keep the game moving quickly and can make it harder for opponents to win possession.

Through Ball

A through ball is a pass played into open space behind the opposing defenders for a teammate to run onto. It is often used to create scoring opportunities.
