Dribbling Techniques

Vision

Keeping your head up while dribbling allows you to observe teammates, opponents, and open spaces on the field. This helps you make better decisions.

Scissors

The scissors move involves moving one foot around the front of the ball to fake a direction. You can then push the ball in the opposite direction.

A body feint uses your shoulders and body movements to make a defender think you are moving in one direction before quickly going another way.
