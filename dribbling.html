Dribbling

Dribbling is the skill of moving with the ball while maintaining control and possession. Soccer players use different dribbling techniques to navigate around defenders, change direction, create space, and maintain possession in different situations during a game.

Dribbling Techniques

Change of Pace

Suddenly changing your speed while dribbling can help you get past defenders. Slowing down and then accelerating quickly can create space.

Scissors

The scissors move involves moving one foot around the front of the ball to fake a direction. You can then push the ball in the opposite direction.

Body Feint

A body feint uses your shoulders and body movements to make a defender think you are moving in one direction before quickly going another way.

Stepovers

Stepovers involve moving one foot around the ball before accelerating in another direction. This can confuse defenders and create opportunities to move forward.

The Ronaldo Chop is a quick change of direction where you use the inside of one foot to move the ball across your body, often while changing direction.

La Croqueta

La Croqueta is a quick movement that uses the inside of one foot to push the ball across to the other foot. It allows a player to change direction smoothly while keeping the ball close.

Elastico

The Elastico is a dribbling move in which the foot quickly moves the ball outward and then back inward in one fluid motion, helping a player change direction and get past a defender.

Reverse Elastico

The Reverse Elastico is the opposite movement of the Elastico. The foot moves the ball inward before quickly flicking it outward, allowing the player to change direction and attempt to get past a defender.
